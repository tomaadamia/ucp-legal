# UCP // Fluent — Pricing

**Last updated: June 8, 2026**

UCP // Fluent is billed through Shopify (the Shopify Billing API). Plans are priced by catalog size (variant count). You can upgrade or downgrade anytime — the new plan replaces the current one and the charge appears in your Shopify admin billing history.

## Subscription plans

| Plan | Monthly | Yearly (10% off) | Catalog | First-payment bonus |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Starter** | $49 / mo | $529.20 / yr | Up to 100 variants | 49 bonus credits |
| **Standard** | $199 / mo | $2,149.20 / yr | Up to 1,000 variants | 199 bonus credits |
| **Pro** | $399 / mo | $4,309.20 / yr | Up to 5,000 variants | 399 bonus credits |
| **Scale** | Custom — contact support | — | 5,000+ variants | Custom |

Every plan includes **Identity** (GS1 GTIN + GPC categories), **Semantic Enrichment**, **Agent-readiness monitoring** (Agent Trust Score), and **AI-agent sales Attribution**.

**Scale:** For stores with 5,000+ variants, a custom Scale plan is available — contact support for pricing. Scale is billed through Shopify like all other plans.

## Usage credits

Credits fund **Product Authentication** (GTIN identity) and **Semantic Enrichment**. They're a separate pay-as-you-go layer on top of your plan.

- **1 credit = $1**, with **volume discounts up to 30%**
- Packs: **10 credits — $10**, **50 credits — $45** (10% off), **100 credits — $85** (15% off)
- Optional **promo codes** and **auto-recharge**
- Each plan includes a one-time **bonus credit grant** on your first payment (equal to the plan's monthly price)

## Billing

All charges — recurring subscriptions and one-time credit purchases — are processed through Shopify Billing. You can cancel or change your plan anytime from your Shopify admin; there are no off-platform invoices.
